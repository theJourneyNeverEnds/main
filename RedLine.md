In the last seven months, I decided to watch all of One Piece. Embarassingly, I actually was put onto the show by the Netflix live action remake. I think I had originally watched all of the East Blue saga back in 2020, but nothing really hooked me. I think I wasn't giving it enough attention, regrettably. I digress. 



The show quickly became one of my favorites, and theoryposting has become a hobby of mine. I don't have much of a life outside of work, so, I need to find something internally fulfilling to occupy my time. I never claimed to be perfect. 



Last night at approximately 03:30 AM pacific time (10:30 UTC), I was riding an absolute *banger* of a high. Super Fog Baja Blazed - that shit was gas. Amidst the haze, I had a strange thought. Not "this is the meaning of life", or "ah, that's the stock to dump my 401k into" - no: "what happened during the Void Century?" This is a long contested debate amongst One Piece readers/viewers, and Oda has been understandably cagey with giving any details. With the current state of the manga (i.e., post-God Valley), it should be noted that the entire theory rests on plenty of assumptions, of which I don't care to name at this time. I believe the theory stands up as is. The reader should *also* note that in order to concoct the retelling of the premonition, I needed to enter the proper headspace I was in *at the time* of the premonition. Reader be advised. 



Roughly 900 years ago, the world was at the precipice of the void century, albeit unknowingly. Major figures we knew to be in play- Imu, Joyboy, and the 20 families (of which, Imu is presumed to be of- don't quote me, i didn't bother to look it up). Here's the big kicker that starts off the theory: 900 years ago, ***SOMETHING*** happened. Whatever this event was was likely the end of the existing world as it was known. The Red Line being a manmade object has been a popular One Piece theory, and that's one of the premises the whole theory hinges on. I don't care if it's agenda. Something happened- the 20 families decided to 'remake' the World Government into the entity it is today. Take a journey with me...



The 20 families agree on the construction of a new system - not entirely of government (although, ya, that happened too), but of ... population control. If the world as it was could be segmented into four equal(ish) sizes, humanity could be contained, monitored, controlled. The Red Line was built intentionally by the 20 founding families. As punishment for whatever the aforementioned 'something' was, humanity was made to work as slaves, constructing the Red Line. Fishmen built the foundation underwater, or possibly with some assistance from Poseidon, and the remainder of the structure was erected via slave labor, or possibly other ancient weapons (at the time of writing this, the power of Uranus is still unknown). The wall is constructed. At this time, the exact dimensions of the Line are unknown, but it is known that Marie Geoise/Marie Jois/Marijoa/Marryjwah (i dont fucking care) sits atop it. I'm not going to make guesses about the size of the city, but it's a fucking kingdom. Gotta be at least *moderately* sized. The wall is constructed. A project of this size would ***absolutely*** rise the sea levels - dramatically. Vegapunk states that the sea levels rose approximately 200m/~656ft during the Void Century, which almost definitely would have flooded and drowned lower-elevation regions. And whose territories may have been amongst the subaquatic? The very builders of the Red Line. Left with no place to go, the builders were likely either executed or taken as slaves by the ruling party. That's entirely conjecture, but so is this whole bit. The construction took approximately 100 years, and once completed, all mentions of a world without the Red Line were scrubbed from history (except Poneglyphs, but this really isn't the point of the theory). 



We need to address the canonical counter to this theory - the Lunarians. What we *do* know is that the Lunarians were hunted down and exterminated by the World Government, with King being the last known surviving member. Even *information* about a Lunarian warrants a 100M reward from the World Government. We know that the Lunarians lived in a "Land of Gods" (canon, WB to Marco). This is later confirmed to be the home of the Lunarians. Picture this - the Lunarians are either among the peoples whose homeland was threatened by the rising seas, or, outside objectors. If the former, they'd presumably have a damn good reason for wanting this all to go to shit. And, with their innate flight ability, they could *literally* settle atop the Red Line. An insurgency, unlawful occupation, straight up 'it was **here** so i took it' behavior, whatever. They set up camp, and decided that's where they'd be. Tbh, the explanation for them *not* being flooded would be the same. I digress yet again. I'm really high right now. 



Naturally, the ruling party wanted dibs on the Red Line. The Lunarians took the spot where the new rulers could look down upon the seas. Maybe metaphorically, maybe allegorically, maybe literally. As punishment, they decided to exterminate the Lunarians. Maybe with ancient weapons, or some domi reversi bullshit. This isn't the strongest point of the theory, but trust me bro. The Lunarians are exterminated, and it's not glamorous. For the record, no genocide is glamorous - I'm just saying "that's what happened, and it wasn't necessarily noteworthy". I digress for the third time. Time passes, and Marie Geoise (im not restating spelling) is built. 



Nothing super noteworthy beyond this. History was erased, and the world as it is known now came to be. As time went on, the World Government continued to quash any efforts of retaining history, and have (canonically) destroyed entire communities for the crime of knowing - not evangelizing*, **KNOWING***. 



That's basically the end of this shit. I'll add more edits as time goes on and more panels drop. Updates will probably be few and far between, but let it be known, I fucking called it.
